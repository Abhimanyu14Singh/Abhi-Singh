TRUSS LAB - HOW TO INSTALL
==========================

Truss Lab is one single file: TrussLab.html
It runs in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
It needs NO internet, NO Python, and NO admin password.


STEP 0 - Unzip first  (important!)
----------------------------------
  * Windows: right-click the zip file -> "Extract All..." -> "Extract".
  * Mac: double-click the zip file. A folder appears next to it.
  Do the next steps INSIDE that unzipped folder.


EASIEST WAY - no installing at all
----------------------------------
  Double-click  TrussLab.html
  It opens in your browser. That's it! You can copy this one file
  anywhere (Desktop, USB stick, email it to a friend) and it still works.


OR - install it with a shortcut
-------------------------------
WINDOWS
  1. Double-click  "Install on Windows.bat"
  2. If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC":
       click "More info", then "Run anyway".
       (This appears for any script downloaded from the internet.)
  3. A black window shows what it did, then Truss Lab opens.
     You now have a "Truss Lab" shortcut on your Desktop and in the Start menu.

MAC
  1. RIGHT-click (or Control-click)  "Install on Mac.command"  -> choose "Open".
  2. If it warns "unidentified developer", click "Open" again.
     (A normal double-click may refuse to run it the first time - that is
      why we right-click.)
     On newer Macs (macOS 15 or later) there may be no "Open" button. Then:
     open System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to the Truss Lab message. Or simply skip the
     installer and use the EASIEST WAY above - it needs none of this.
  3. Truss Lab opens. You now have "Truss Lab.html" on your Desktop.
     The app itself lives in your home folder: Applications > Truss Lab.

LINUX
  1. Open a terminal in this folder and type:   sh install-linux.sh
  2. "Truss Lab" appears in your applications menu (and on the Desktop).


FIRST TIME USING IT
-------------------
  * It opens with a sample bridge (a Pratt truss) already solved.
  * Click the  ?  button (top right) for a quick how-to. Press Esc or the X
    to close it.
  * Tools on the left: Joint -> Member -> Support -> Load.
  * The "Insights" tab explains which member carries the most force and which
    member controls the deflection - and why.


TO UNINSTALL
------------
  Windows: delete the folder  %LOCALAPPDATA%\TrussLab
           (paste that into the File Explorer address bar), then delete the
           "Truss Lab" shortcuts from the Desktop and Start menu.
  Mac:     delete  Applications > Truss Lab  in your home folder, and the
           "Truss Lab.html" shortcut on the Desktop.
  Linux:   rm -r ~/.local/share/trusslab ~/.local/share/applications/trusslab.desktop


YOUR WORK
---------
  Use the Save button to download your truss as a .json file, and Load to open
  it again later. (The app does not keep drawings when you close the tab.)
